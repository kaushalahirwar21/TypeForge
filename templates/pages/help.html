Ergonomics & Typing Posture Checklist

  • ✓ Seat Height: Adjust your chair so your thighs are horizontal and feet flat on the floor.
  • ✓ Elbow Angle: Keep elbows bent at approximately 90 degrees with forearms parallel to your desk.
  • ✓ Floating Wrists: Do not rest your wrists heavily on the table or keyboard edge while typing; let them hover gently.
  • ✓ Eye Level: Position the top third of your monitor at eye level to prevent neck strain.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to learn touch typing?

Most learners see substantial gains in 2 to 3 weeks of daily 15-minute practice. By completing all 13 levels on TypeRise, beginners typically reach 40–60 WPM, and intermediate typists frequently break 80+ WPM.

Should I prioritize speed or accuracy first?

Always prioritize accuracy first. Typing fast with frequent errors slows you down significantly due to corrections. Once your accuracy consistently exceeds 95–98%, speed develops naturally and effortlessly.

Can I turn off the sound effects or virtual keyboard?

Yes! The lesson screen includes instant toggles for sound, virtual keyboard, and hand guide. You can also customize your global defaults in the Settings page.
