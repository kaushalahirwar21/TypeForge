Our Mission

Empowering Global Digital Fluency

TypeForge was created to transform how people interact with computers. Typing is the fundamental input channel of modern work, study, and creative expression.

Why Touch Typing Matters

When you hunt and peck, your brain is forced to split attention between your thoughts and the physical keyboard. By committing the QWERTY keyboard layout to muscle memory, typing becomes subconscious—leaving 100% of your cognitive bandwidth free for problem-solving, creative writing, and critical reasoning.

An increase in typing speed from 30 WPM to 70 WPM saves the average computer worker over 21 working days per year in pure input time alone.

The TypeForge Pedagogical Approach

🎯

Deliberate Practice

We don't just dump random words on your screen. Each of our 13 levels introduces specific finger-key pairs, solidifying spatial relationships before moving up to words, capitalization, numbers, and symbols.

⚡

Instant Multi-Sensory Feedback

Visual colors, live finger tracking diagrams, and subtle synthesized auditory clicks instantly signal correct and incorrect strikes, accelerating neural reinforcement.

🧠

Adaptive Weak-Key Elimination

Our algorithm tracks every character mistake you make and pinpoints the exact keys causing deceleration, producing targeted warmups to turn weaknesses into strengths.
