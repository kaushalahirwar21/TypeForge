Foundation Tutorial Touch Typing Posture & Anchors

Mastering Physical Touch Typing Habits

1. Never Look Down at Your Keyboard

Touch typing relies entirely on tactile muscle memory. If you glance at your fingers, you break the neural reflex loop.

2. Find the F and J Guide Bumps

Both keys have small physical ridges. Your index fingers should naturally seek and anchor on these bumps without looking.

3. Floating Wrists & 90-Degree Elbows

Do not rest your wrists heavily on the table while typing. Keep elbows bent at 90 degrees and wrists floating lightly.

4. The 8 Home Keys

Left hand: A, S, D, F. Right hand: J, K, L, Semicolon (;). Thumbs rest gently over the Space Bar.
